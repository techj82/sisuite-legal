# SISuite  
### Service Intake Suite — a reusable, AI-powered automation platform for service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, home services, etc.) that handles the full customer lifecycle: lead intake, AI-driven enrichment/qualification, scheduling and reminders, technician dispatch, CRM integration, and invoicing/payments.

---

## Opt-In Form Example

This is a sample opt-in flow demonstrating how users provide consent to receive SMS authentication messages from SISuite.

### **Phone Number (Optional)**
`[  Enter your phone number  ]`

---

By adding your phone number, you accept the **SISuite Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** and agree to receive **transactional SMS authentication messages** from SISuite.  
Message frequency varies.  
Reply **HELP** for help or **STOP** to opt out.  
Msg&Data rates may apply.

---

## Compliance Notes (Twilio Requirements)

- **Brand/business name must match the campaign**  
- **Phone number collection must be optional**  
- **Opt-in language must match the campaign use case**  
- **Transactional — No checkbox required**

## Example Opt-In Method (SMS Keyword)

Users may also opt in by texting **SISUITE** to **3334445555**.  
By texting SISUITE, users agree to receive one-time passcodes (OTP) and login verification messages for authentication.
