# SISuite SMS Opt-In Call-to-Action

To receive authentication codes from SISuite, text SISUITE to 3334445555.

By texting SISUITE, you agree to receive SMS messages from SISuite for login and identity verification. 
Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance.

This page serves as the publicly reachable opt-in call-to-action required for A2P campaign registration.
