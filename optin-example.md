# SISuite SMS Opt-In Example

To receive authentication codes from SISuite, text **SISUITE** to **3334445555**.

By texting SISUITE, you consent to receive one-time passcodes (OTP) for login and identity verification.
Reply **STOP** to opt out. Reply **HELP** for assistance.

This page is provided as a sample opt-in call-to-action for A2P campaign registration.
