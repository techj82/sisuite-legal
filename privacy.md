# SISuite Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 21, 2026

SISuite (“we,” “our,” “us”) provides software tools for service intake, scheduling, workflow automation, and account authentication. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, including your mobile phone number.

## Information We Collect
- Phone number provided during account creation or login
- Name, email address, and other account details you choose to provide
- Authentication logs (time, date, and verification status)
- Device and browser information

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Authenticate your identity
- Send one-time passcodes (OTP) when requested
- Secure your account
- Provide customer support
- Improve platform functionality

## Mobile Number Privacy
- We do not share, sell, or transfer your mobile number to third parties.
- Your mobile number is used only for authentication and account-related messaging.

## Message Frequency
Authentication messages are sent only when requested by you. You will not receive marketing or promotional messages.

## Messaging Disclosure
Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.

## Opt-Out
Reply STOP to stop receiving authentication messages.

## Help
Reply HELP for assistance or contact us at support@serviceintakesuite.com.

## Data Security
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page.
